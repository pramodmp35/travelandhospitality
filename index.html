Who we are

Not a booking site.
A design studio.

STACKLY started in 2014 with a simple frustration: travel had become transactional. We believed it should be crafted.

Today, our team of 24 designers, guides, and hotel obsessives work out of three studios. We don't use algorithms. We use our own feet, our own eyes, and a very stubborn refusal to book a hotel we haven't personally slept in.
