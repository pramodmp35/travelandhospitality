Our story

From a one-room studio

Every company has a first trip. Ours started with two backpacks, a borrowed laptop and one very stubborn belief.

2014

Two founders, one rented desk

Mira and Dev set up STACKLY in a shared studio in Panaji. First booking: a three-night Goa weekend for a friend of a friend.

2016

Our first international chapter

We curated our first curated trip to Sri Lanka — 14 travellers, 9 days, zero itineraries. It sold out in 6 hours.

2019

1,000th happy traveller

The team grew to 11, and we opened our second studio in Bengaluru. The concierge line went 24/7 for the first time.

2022

Building back, slower

After two quiet years, we relaunched with a smaller footprint, longer stays and a renewed focus on local hosts.

2025

12,000 journeys and counting

A team of 24 across 3 studios. Same belief, same stubbornness, a much bigger map.

What drives us

Vision & mission

Our vision

A world where travel is a craft, not a checkout.

We want every trip to feel like it was made for one person — because it was. No templates, no algorithm, no “customers also viewed”. Just slow, careful, human-made journeys that people remember for decades.

Our mission

Design one-of-one trips, without the luxury markup.

We pair each traveller with a dedicated designer, use the same small hotels we’ve personally stayed in, and price everything transparently. Honest travel, at human scale.
